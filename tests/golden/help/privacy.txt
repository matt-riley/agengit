USAGE:
    agit privacy scan [OPTIONS]

DESCRIPTION:
    Scan reachable captured content for sensitive data without printing secret values.

OPTIONS:
    --json
        Render privacy scan findings as structured JSON.
    -h, --help
        Display this help and exit.

EXAMPLES:
    # scan captured content for sensitive data
    agit privacy scan
    # emit machine-readable privacy findings
    agit privacy scan --json
